Your answers came through. Nothing else is needed from you right now.

What happens next

  1. I read what you sent.
    Every answer, including the parts you probably thought didn't matter. That's the whole point of asking.
  2. I email you within 24 hours.
    We'll book a 30-minute call — the one that decides whether this is actually worth your money. I'd rather find out it isn't on the call than after you've paid.
  3. Then your bot goes live.
    My side does the building. You get a Telegram bot with your name on it, ready to take dictation from the truck.

One thing you can do now

If you don't already have Telegram installed, grab it — that's where your agent will live. It's free, and it's the same app on your phone and your computer, so a call you log from the truck shows up at the desk.

Nothing to sign up for beyond that, and no app I built that you have to learn.

Especially useful thing to have ready for the call: the equipment you see most often. Brands, model numbers, the stuff you keep re-learning. That's what your agent builds its memory around, and the more of it you can name, the sharper it is out of the gate.
