📋 Statute Quick-Compare

🏢 Condo (FS 718)
  • Unit owners own units + share common elements
  • Strong lien authority (super lien for 6 mo assessments)
  • Reserve studies required for major components
  • Election by unit, not shareholder
🤝 Co-op (FS 719)
  • Owners own shares in corporation
  • Corporation owns property outright
  • Greater eviction authority than condos
  • Voting by share, not unit
🏡 HOA (FS 720)
  • Owners own lots + mandatory membership
  • More limited lien vs. condos
  • Arbitration required for covenant disputes (720.311)
  • Homestead protection for owner-occupied

✅ Daily/Weekly Compliance Checklist

  • Annual meeting notices due (60-day / 14-day windows)
  • Budget delivery deadlines approaching
  • Insurance renewals within 60 days — COI on file?
  • Open violations past compliance deadline
  • Fine hearings scheduled for this week
  • Records inspection requests pending
  • Board meeting minutes from last meeting filed
  • CAM license CE credits on track (16 hrs biennial)
  • Reserve study update due
  • Vendor insurance certs expiring
