# Florida CAM Compliance Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for FS 718 (Condos), FS 719 (Cooperatives), and FS 720 (HOAs).

---

## Meeting & Notice Deadlines

| Requirement | Condo (718) | HOA (720) |
|---|---|---|
| Annual meeting notice | **60 days** (718.112(2)(d)1) | **14 days** (720.306(1)) |
| Budget delivery before meeting | **14 days** (718.112(2)(f)) | Before fiscal year (720.303(6)) |
| Board meeting notice | **48 hours** posted | **48 hours** posted |
| Record inspection response | **10 business days** (718.111(12)) | **10 business days** (720.303(4)) |
| Notice of lien intent | **30 days** (718.116) | Per governing docs |
| Vote/amenity suspension | **90 days** delinquent (718.303(4)) | **90 days** delinquent (720.305) |

## Fine Schedule

| Item | Limit | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Per day | **$100 max** | 718.303(3) / 720.305(2) |
| Cumulative cap | **$1,000** (unless docs allow more) | 718.303(3) |
| Fine committee | **No board members** | 718.303(3) |
| Right to hearing | Written request, present evidence | 718.303 / 720.305 |

### Violation Enforcement Pipeline

**1st Notice** → compliance period → **2nd Notice (Final Warning)** → response period → **Fine Hearing** → committee decision → **Fine Imposed**

Cured at any stage. Escalate to attorney if unpaid/unresolved.

## Statute Quick-Compare

**Condo (FS 718):** Unit owners own units + share common elements. Strong lien authority (super lien for 6 months assessments). Reserve studies required for major components. Election by unit.

**Co-op (FS 719):** Owners own shares in corporation. Corporation owns property outright. Greater eviction authority. Voting by share.

**HOA (FS 720):** Owners own lots + mandatory membership. More limited lien authority vs condos. Arbitration required for covenant disputes (720.311). Homestead protection for owner-occupied.

## Financial Reporting Thresholds (718.111(13))

| Annual Revenue | Required Report |
|---|---|
| $150K – $300K | **Compiled** financial statement |
| $300K – $500K | **Reviewed** financial statement |
| > $500K | **Audited** financial statement |

## Daily/Weekly Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Annual meeting notices due (60-day / 14-day windows)
- [ ] Budget delivery deadlines approaching
- [ ] Insurance renewals within 60 days — COI on file?
- [ ] Open violations past compliance deadline
- [ ] Fine hearings scheduled for this week
- [ ] Records inspection requests pending
- [ ] Board meeting minutes from last meeting filed
- [ ] CAM license CE credits on track (16 hrs biennial)
- [ ] Reserve study update due
- [ ] Vendor insurance certs expiring
